What Turnia is

Turnia lets healthcare professionals organise their shifts in groups, keep personal events, share their calendar with other users and offer shifts for other members of their group to take, keeping a record of every transfer.

Turnia is an organisational tool. It is not the official roster of any hospital, health centre or employer, and it does not replace the approval procedures those employers require. A shift swap recorded in Turnia is an agreement between the members involved: it is up to them to confirm it with whoever is responsible for the roster at their workplace and to make sure it complies with their working-time rules, collective agreements and labour law. The Service Provider does not check any of this and is not responsible for a shift going uncovered, for a swap that the employer does not accept, or for decisions made on the basis of information shown in the Application.

Groups, admins and shift swaps

  • Whoever creates a group becomes its admin. Admins define the group's event types, decide who joins, can remove members and can delete any event in the group.
  • Anyone with a group's invitation code can ask to join; an admin must accept the request unless the group was set up to accept everyone with the code. Share invitation codes only with the people who should join.
  • You are responsible for the events you create and for the accuracy of the information you enter. Members of a group see the group's events and their transfer history.
  • Taking a shift offered for swap moves it to you immediately and is recorded in its history. When two members try to take the same shift, the first request to reach the server wins.
  • If you leave a group, or an admin removes you, while you still hold events in it, you keep read-only access to your own events there.
