What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

Turnia is a tool for healthcare teams to organise their shifts and swap them. To use it you must sign in with a Google or Apple account. The Application processes the following information:

  • Account: your email address and the identifier of the account you signed in with (Google or Apple), managed by Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile: your name, your username and the avatar you choose (an animal icon and a background colour).
  • Groups: the groups you create or join, your role in them (member or admin), and your requests to join.
  • Shifts and events: the group events you create, take or offer for swap, together with the history of who transferred each shift to whom; and your personal events and event types, including any notes you add to them.
  • Calendar sharing: the list of users you grant access to your calendar.
  • Preferences: the colours you choose for event types and whether notifications are enabled.
  • Device and push: a push notification token for each device you sign in on.
  • Subscription: whether you have an active premium subscription, as verified with the Google Play or App Store receipt. Payment details are handled entirely by Google or Apple; the Service Provider never receives your card data.

This information is used only to provide the Application: showing your calendar and your groups' calendars, keeping the record of shift swaps, notifying the people involved in a change, and managing your subscription. The Service Provider does not sell your personal data and does not send you marketing communications.

Who can see your information inside the Application?

  • Any signed-in user of the Application can see your name, username and avatar, so that they can find you, and the list of users you share your calendar with.
  • Members of a group you belong to can see the group's events, including yours and their swap history. Group event documents never contain your private notes.
  • Users you grant access to your calendar can see all your events, across groups, including your personal events and their notes. You can revoke this access at any time.
  • Your email address, push tokens, preferences and subscription status are visible only to you.
